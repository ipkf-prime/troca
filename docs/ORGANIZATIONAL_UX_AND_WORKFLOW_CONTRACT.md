# Organizational UX and Workflow Contract

- User-facing dates are Jalali; database dates remain Gregorian.
- Organization, unit, and position selectors use full hierarchical paths.
- Explicit routing rules override organization defaults.
- Organization default routing mode is either free or structural.
- Manual rules are group-based: named group, members, recipients, operations, validity.
- Outgoing correspondence stages are typing, approval, signature, registration, dispatch.
- Registration and dispatch are secretariat responsibilities.
- Dispatch can be internal inbox delivery or external immutable PDF/JPG output.
- Signed and registered snapshots are immutable.
